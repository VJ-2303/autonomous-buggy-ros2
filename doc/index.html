Campus Car Live Simulation

Click the map to place a rider: first click = pickup, second click = drop. The car drives in real time, replans when you add someone while it is moving, and drains battery as it goes. Map is built from your hand-drawn campus sketch (not to scale; 1 drawing pixel = 0.6 m, to be calibrated). Several riders can share the same pickup or drop place.

Rules: 4 seats, 0.01 % battery per metre, 20 % reserve, detour limit 1.5x direct, car speed 5 m/s (sim time).

Click on the map to choose a pickup point.
